Regia. — Internal analytics tool

Regia is a private, internal desktop tool used by Axiom Labs to read the public performance statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, follower count) of TikTok accounts that we own and operate, in order to plan our own content.

It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up and is used only with accounts owned by the operator.
