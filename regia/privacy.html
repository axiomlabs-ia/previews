Regia. — Privacy Policy

Regia runs locally on the operator's computer. It collects only the data needed to measure the performance of accounts owned by Axiom Labs.

  • Data read via TikTok APIs: basic profile info (open id, display name, avatar), profile statistics (followers, likes, video count) and public video metadata and counts (views, likes, comments, shares, publish time, duration).
  • Purpose: internal content planning and performance tracking only.
  • Storage: on the operator's computer, in a local database. Access tokens are stored locally and never shared.
  • Sharing: data is not sold or shared with third parties and is not used for advertising.
  • Retention and deletion: data is kept while the account is connected; it can be deleted at any time by disconnecting the account or deleting the local database. To request deletion write to info@stefanodemartis.com.
  • Third-party users: the tool does not collect data about viewers or other TikTok users.

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Operator: Stefano Demartis (Axiom Labs) · Contact: info@stefanodemartis.com
